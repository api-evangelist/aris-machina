# Aris Machina

> Aris Machina builds AI-powered industrial software for advanced manufacturing — "industrial superintelligence" that unifies design, production, and operations through AI agents and a shared semantic knowledge model. Backed by Earlybird; offices in Stockholm, Bangalore, and California. No public developer API surface, OpenAPI specification, or SDKs are published at this time.

## Products
- [Protos](https://www.arismachina.com/protos): AI co-engineer for R&D teams — rapid design validation and simulation.
- [Gemba](https://www.arismachina.com/#gemba): Shopfloor operational agents for real-time equipment diagnostics.
- [Ontology Toolkit](https://www.arismachina.com/#ontology): Knowledge-integration layer bridging MES, ERP, and PLM data.
